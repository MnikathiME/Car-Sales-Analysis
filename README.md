Bright Motors — Car Sales Analysis

A data analytics case study analysing car sales data for Bright Motors to help a new Head of Sales make smarter business decisions.

[Live Dashboard](https://motor-vision-analytics.lovable.app)

What This Project Is About

Bright Motors needed insights from their historical sales data (2006–2015) to grow the business. As a Junior Data Analyst, I cleaned and analysed 1,000+ vehicle transactions and presented findings and recommendations to guide their sales and inventory strategy.

Tools Used

Databricks — data cleaning and analysis
Google Looker and ChatGPT — visualisations
Canva — presentation design
Lovable — interactive dashboard
Excel - data reading(raw data)
Miro - project planning

Key Findings

Best-selling car makes:
- Nissan led with 10.8% of sales
- BMW (9.8%) and Infiniti (9.4%) were top premium brands
- Asian and premium brands together made up 44% of all sales

Top models: G Sedan, Altima, 3 Series, Accord, Civic

Colour preferences:
- 70% of buyers chose neutral colours (white, black, gray)
- Black interiors were the most popular across all models

Price trends:
- 80% of sales were budget cars under $15K
- Premium cars ($30K+) had lower volume but higher profit margins

Sales over time:
- 2012 was the peak year with around 180 units sold
- There was a sharp drop in 2015 that needs further investigation

Regional performance:
- California made up over 80% of all sales — a concentration risk

Recommendations

- Stock smarter — focus on the top 5 models in neutral colours with low mileage
- Expand geographically — open a Texas or Florida location to reduce California dependency
- Grow the premium segment — launch a Certified Pre-Owned programme for BMW, Mercedes, and Infiniti
- Build financial partnerships — work with banks and lease companies to reduce acquisition costs
